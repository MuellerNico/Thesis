\section{Performance and scaling analysis}
\label{sec:performance}

In this chapter we conduct a small performance and scaling analysis of the new MHD implementation in SPH-EXA. The first goal is to verify that parallel scaling efficiency is upheld compared to the pure hydrodynamic code, which will serve as a baseline here. Secondly, we inspect the relative computational overhead and ensure that we observe it in places where it is expected and to a degree that is reasonable. We also compare the performance of the two main resistivity schemes discussed throughout this paper and quantify the additional overhead induced through reconstruction by ARSLR (§\ref{sec:ar}). 

In all performance experiments the magnetized Sedov blast test case is used (§\ref{sec:sedov}) with its non-magnetized hydrodynamic equivalent as baseline. Performance is measured in execution time per iteration (time step). This way, the effect of the MHD-specific physical time step constraint (§\ref{sec:timeint}), which varies strongly between different test cases, is eliminated. 

All runs are performed on nVidia RTX 4090 GPUs with 24 GB of VRAM each.
