\begin{abstract}
% \vspace{5cm}
Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) is a model to describe the magnetic behavior of plasmas and has applications in astrophysics and many other fields. The numerical simulation of MHD problems at scale is therefore of great interest. In this thesis paper, we present a complete and verified implementation of MHD in SPH-EXA. This includes a verification suite of standard MHD tests to allow comparison with other state-of-the-art software. We take a close look at artificial resistivity and explore different implementation schemes, including a novel reconstruction-based method recently proposed for artificial viscosity to reduce overall dissipation. Finally, we leverage the scalability of SPH-EXA to simulate subsonic magnetic turbulence with over 4 billion particles. 

\end{abstract}
