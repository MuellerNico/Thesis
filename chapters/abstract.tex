In this thesis paper, we present a complete and verified implementation of MHD in SPH-EXA. This includes a suite of standard MHD tests to allow comparison with other state-of-the-art SPMHD codes. We take a close look at artificial resistivity and explore different implementation schemes, including a reconstruction-based method recently proposed for artificial viscosity. Finally, we leverage the scalability of SPH-EXA to simulate subsonic magnetic turbulence with over 4 billion particles.
