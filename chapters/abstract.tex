Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) ...

In this Thesis work, we present a complete and verified implementation of MHD in SPH-EXA. This includes a suite of standard MHD tests to allow comparison with other state-of-the-art SPMHD software. We take a close look at artificial resistivity and explore different implementation schemes, including a reconstruction-based method recently proposed for artificial viscosity. Finally, we leverage the scalability of SPH-EXA to simulate subsonic MHD turbulence at resolution of $1000^3$ particles. 

\end{abstract}
